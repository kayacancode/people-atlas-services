People Data Sync brings Gmail activity, meeting history, and people notes together so you can understand your relationships, find reasons to reconnect, and explore personal and shared networks.

Get the Obsidian pluginOpen the People graph

Bring context into your vault

The desktop Obsidian plugin creates people and company notes, tracks communication activity and relationship scores, and provides timelines and graph views. Your notes and local plugin data stay in your vault unless you choose to use a connected cloud feature.

Explore personal and shared networks

The optional hosted People app lets you explore a personal graph and networks you belong to. Uploading a graph, importing a connected account, and sharing with a network are separate actions with different data flows.

Why we request Google access

Google sign-in identifies your account. Gmail read access supports contact and conversation metadata sync; optional hosted email-context features also process eligible message content after you enable them. Calendar access supports meeting history; Contacts access supports contact names, photos, and profiles. Optional booking features request additional calendar permissions. The permissions shown by Google can be broader than the data read during normal sync. Review the consent screen before connecting.

Your judgment stays central

Recency can tell you when a relationship has gone quiet. Context can help you understand why reconnecting might matter. Your judgment determines what happens next.

AI enrichment and connected agent features are optional. Their processing can involve third-party services. Read our privacy policy for what is accessed, where it is stored, how sharing works, and how to disconnect or request deletion.
